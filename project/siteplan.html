Site Name & Logo

TrailFinder

Site Name: TrailFinder (Nature & Hiking Trail Planner)

Optional Domain Availability: trailfinder-planner.org

Reason for Name: The name "TrailFinder" is concise, memorable, and directly communicates the site's primary utility—helping outdoor enthusiasts discover, filter, and plan hiking routes suited to their skill level.

Site Purpose

The primary purpose of TrailFinder is to serve as an interactive outdoor guide for local hiking trails and nature parks. The website provides detailed route specifications (difficulty, distance, and estimated duration), high-resolution imagery, and dynamic search tools.

Key services and features provided to visitors include:

  • An interactive visual catalog of curated hiking routes.
  • Dynamic JavaScript filtering by difficulty level (Easy, Moderate, Hard).
  • Personalized trail bookmarking powered by browser localStorage.
  • A signup form for guided group hikes and information requests.
  • A dedicated references page citing image and data sources.

Target Audience Scenarios

Scenario 1

Beginner Hiker

"I want to take my family on a weekend walk, but we need an easy trail with minimal elevation gain that takes under 2 hours. How can I quickly find matching routes and save them for later?"

Site Solution: The user can use the difficulty filter controls on the catalog page to filter for "Easy" routes, inspect distance and estimated times, and click "Save to Favorites" to store choices locally in their browser.

Scenario 2

Experienced Backpacker / Group Trail Seeker

"I am looking for a challenging mountain route with scenic views and want to sign up for an upcoming guided weekend expedition. Where can I register?"

Site Solution: The user filters for "Hard" trails, reads route specifics, and fills out the integrated guided hike registration form to lock in a spot on the next group trek.
